Housing Guide

If you're looking to move to Liverpool, whether you're after a city-centre flat, a little family home, or a house with a bit of garden space, the city's got options for every budget. We've even had a mooch ourselves and gathered some information on average prices for yous to have a look at. Below you'll find a breakdown of house prices and rental prices across various areas, from the buzzy city centre to quieter suburban spots.

City Centre

The city centre is ideal for those who want to be close to the action, with plenty of shops, bars, restaurants, and public transport options. Expect higher prices here for both buying and renting. Your options for the city centre are mainly apartments and studio flats, if you're after a house, you'll probably have to stray off a bit from the city centre kidda.

Average Purchase Prices

  • Studio Apartments: £120,000 - £160,000
  • 1-Bed Apartments: £140,000 - £180,000
  • 2-Bed Apartments: £200,000 - £240,000

Average Rental Prices

  • Studio Apartments: £650 - £750 pcm
  • 1-Bed Apartments: £850 - £1,000 pcm
  • 2-Bed Apartments: £900 - £1,200 pcm

Suburbs - Aintree, Fazakerley, Walton

If you're looking for a quieter area with good transport links into the city centre, the suburbs are a good bet. Areas like Aintree, Fazakerley, and Walton offer more affordable housing options, with good access to parks and schools.

Average Purchase Prices

  • 2-Bed Houses: £120,000 - £160,000
  • 3-Bed Houses: £160,000 - £220,000
  • 4-Bed Houses: £270,000 - £370,000

Average Rental Prices

  • 2-Bed Houses: £750- £850
  • 3-Bed Houses: £850 - £950
  • 4-Bed Houses: £1,100 - £1,250

Furrther From the City - Woolton, Childwall, Formby

For families looking for more space and a quieter lifestyle, areas like Woolton Childwall, and Formby offer excellent local schools, parks, and more spacious homes. Prices are slightly higher than in Bootle and Aintree but still affordable compared to city centre properties.

Average Purchase Prices

  • 2-Bed Houses: £150,000 - £200,000
  • 3-Bed Houses: £200,000 - £300,000
  • 4-Bed Houses: £300,000 - £400,000

Average Rental Prices

  • 2-Bed Houses: £850- £950
  • 3-Bed Houses: £950 - £1,100
  • 4-Bed Houses: £1,250 - £1,500

Areas for Students - Kensington & Edge Hill

Popular among students and young professionals, areas like Kensington and Edge Hill offer affordable housing options with easy access to universities, shops, and public transport, as well as being close to the City Centre.

Average Purchase Prices

  • Studio Apartments: £80,000 - £100,000
  • 1-Bed Apartments: £90,000 - £110,000
  • 2-Bed Apartments: £120,000 - £150,000

Average Rental Prices

  • Studio Apartments: £500 - £650 pcm
  • 1-Bed Apartments: £650 - £750 pcm
  • 2-Bed Apartments: £750 - £850 pcm
