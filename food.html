Restaurants & Places to Eat

City Centre Restaurants

Burger & Chicken Spots

  • Red Dog Saloon – American BBQ joint with messy wings, smoked meats and burgers. Big portions.
  • Almost Famous – Loud, quirky burger place with wild toppings and loaded fries.
  • Byron Burgers – Sleek and simple, classic burgers with a few craft beer options.
  • Fat Hippo – Big stacked burgers and dirty fries, proper comfort food.
  • Honest Burgers – British-style burgers with rosemary salted chips – decent if you like quality ingredients.
  • Patterson's – Fried chicken heaven, American diner vibes and old school tunes playing.
  • Archie's – Pink neon burger & shake spot, known for over-the-top sides and Insta-friendly vibes.
  • Five Guys – You know the drill – simple but banging burgers, chips and refills galore.

Pizza & Italian

  • Villa Romana – Cosy Italian with proper traditional pasta and a homely feel.
  • Gusto – Stylish restaurant on the docks with classic Italian dishes and cocktails.
  • What's Cooking? – Mix of American and Italian with pizza, ribs, burgers and a relaxed vibe.
  • Smoke & Dough – All-you-can-eat grilled meats brought to your table, plus pizza and sides. Bit different!
  • Nightcrawler Pizza – Late-night pizza bar with New York slices and a bit of an underground buzz.
  • Pizza Punks – Build-your-own pizzas with unlimited toppings – dead fun and tasty.
  • American Pizza Slice – Huge NYC-style slices sold individually – quick and boss for a snack.
  • Bella Italia – Classic Italian chain, nothing too fancy but gets the job done.
  • Rudy’s Neapolitan Pizza – Authentic soft-base pizzas, proper wood-fired and freshly made.
  • Crust – Loads of dough choices (even black charcoal base), great pastas too.

Asian Cuisine

  • Maggie Fu – Fast, flavour-packed Asian street food with massive portions – the salt & pepper chicken and spicy noodles are top tier.
  • Miyagi – Japanese-inspired street food with bao buns, ramen, cocktails and more.
  • Wagamama – Big menu with noodles, katsu curries and clean Asian flavours.
  • Etsu – Hidden gem for proper sushi, donburi and traditional Japanese eats.
  • Bon Bon Bakery – Casual Chinese bakery & restaurant – pork buns, bubble tea, proper tasty.
  • Chamber 36 – Pan-Asian with cocktails, small plates, and a slick modern vibe.
  • Big Bowl Noodle Bar – No-fuss, cheap and cheerful spot for pho, udon and rice dishes.

Tapas & Mediterranean

  • Lunya – Catalan deli and tapas bar – chorizo, cheese and wine for days.
  • Salt House Tapas – Classy modern tapas with cocktails and good veggie options.
  • La Parilla – Spanish/Mexican fusion with generous portions and buzzing vibe.
  • Bakchich – Lebanese street food with grilled meats, wraps, hummus and colour everywhere.

Vegan & Vegetarian Friendly

  • Down the Hatch – Full-on comfort food like vegan burgers, loaded fries and deep-fried everything.
  • Frost Burgers – 100% vegan fast food spot with filthy burgers and shakes.
  • The Egg Café – Old school veggie café above a record shop – soups, bakes, and boss cakes.
  • Maray – Amazing small plates with loads of veggie/vegan options, inspired by Middle Eastern flavours.

Steak & Grill

  • Smoke & Dough – American-Italian grill with a twist – all-you-can-eat meats served straight to your table with a proper boss selection of sides and sauces.
  • Macello Meathouse – Juicy steaks, mixed grills, and good sides – proper feast.
  • Cowshed – Quality steakhouse with a boss wine list and cosy interior.
  • Hawksmoor – Higher-end British steak restaurant in the old India Buildings – ideal for a splurge.
  • Miller & Carter – Steak chain with smart service and massive platters.

Modern & Mixed Cuisine

  • Bold Street Coffee – Known for brunch but also does good toasties, pancakes, and craft coffee.
  • Moose Coffee – Big American-style breakfasts, pancake stacks and strong coffee.
  • The Florist – Floral interiors, modern British food and stunning cocktails – very Instagrammable.
  • Baltic Market – Street food hall with rotating vendors – everything from pizza to halloumi fries.
  • Duke Street Market – Fancy food court vibes, loads of cuisines under one roof – proper good buzz.

Restaurants Outside the City Centre

Smithdown Road & Wavertree

  • Spitroast – Proper roast dinners, wraps and healthy options – really popular with students.
  • Big Lola's Taqueria – Mexican vibes with tacos, burritos and banging cocktails – small but sound.
  • Naked Noodle – Chill Asian street food place, loads of noodle bowls and bubble tea.
  • Craft Taproom – Not just craft beers – loads of rotating kitchen takeovers doing burgers, tacos etc.

Lark Lane

  • Maranto's – American-Italian spot in a quirky building. Good pasta, burgers and cocktails.
  • Chilli Banana – Thai restaurant with authentic curries and noodles, proper underrated.
  • The Moon & Pea – Cosy café-style spot with breakfast, brunch and homemade dishes.

Allerton & Woolton

  • Allerton Manor – Upscale restaurant in a stunning old manor house, set by a golf course – great for Sunday roasts, steaks and classy dinners.
  • The Interesting Eating Company – Great for pancakes, breakfast and light bites. Kid-friendly too.
  • Leaf on Allerton – Lovely brunch spot with loads of teas and healthy options – same vibe as the Bold St one.
  • Casa Italia (Woolton) – Family-run Italian that’s proper old school – warm service and big portions.
