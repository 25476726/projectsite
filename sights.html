Sightseeing around Liverpool

From iconic buildings to hidden gems, Liverpool’s packed with places to explore. Whether you’re into history, views, street art or the waterfront, there’s always something boss to see.

Landmarks & Architecture

These are the proper head-turners – big names, stunning buildings and proper Liverpool heritage.

  • Royal Liver Building – One of the Three Graces on the Pier Head, topped with the famous Liver Birds.
  • St George’s Hall – Grand neoclassical building right by Lime Street – boss for concerts and events too.
  • Liverpool Cathedral (Anglican) – One of the biggest in the world, stunning inside and you can head up the tower for mega views.
  • Metropolitan Cathedral – The big circular one locals call “Paddy’s Wigwam” – totally different look but just as striking.
  • Albert Dock – UNESCO-listed waterfront site with museums, food spots, and gorgeous architecture.

Waterfront Walks & Views

Perfect for a wander – especially on a clear day. Grab an ice cream or just soak in the views.

  • Royal Albert Dock – Ideal for photos, people-watching and popping into the Tate or Maritime Museum.
  • Pier Head – Home of the Three Graces and the Beatles statue – right by the Mersey.
  • Otterspool Promenade – Long riverside walk with a cracking view out across the water, proper chilled vibe.
  • Sefton Park’s Lake – Not quite the Mersey, but boss for a stroll around the water and a coffee at the café.
  • Ferry Across the Mersey – Classic tune, classic view – you’ve got to do it at least once!

Culture & Museums

Dead good for a day out – most of these are free and proper interesting too.

  • World Museum – Dinosaurs, space, Ancient Egypt – one for the big kids and little ones.
  • Museum of Liverpool – All about the city’s history, footy, fashion and music – proper Scouse stuff.
  • Tate Liverpool – Modern art gallery down by the docks, always something interesting going on.
  • International Slavery Museum – Powerful and important look at Liverpool’s role in global history.
  • British Music Experience – From The Beatles to Bowie, it’s all about the sounds of Britain.

Street Art & Murals

Dead colourful and always changing – Liverpool’s got loads of mad pieces dotted around the city.

  • Baltic Triangle – Packed with huge murals, indie cafés and bars – great for a wander and a few photos.
  • Jürgen Klopp Mural (Jordan Street) – The gaffer himself immortalised in paint. Worth a look if you're near Cains Brewery.
  • The Beatles Street Art (Seel Street) – Colourful tribute to the Fab Four just off the main nightlife strip.
  • Hope Street’s Wings Mural – Insta-famous pair of angel wings on the wall near the Everyman Theatre.
  • Stanley Dock Graffiti Wall – Slightly more lowkey, but loads of edgy pieces along the warehouses and canals.

Parks & Green Spots

Boss when the sun’s out or even just for a quiet walk to clear your head.

  • Sefton Park – Massive Victorian park with a boating lake, palm house, and loads of space for a kickabout or picnic.
  • Princes Park – Just down the road from Sefton, quieter and more chilled – lovely little lake too.
  • Calderstones Park – Has a beautiful Japanese garden, ancient stones and a cracking café in the mansion house.
  • Everton Park – One of the best viewpoints in the city, especially at sunset – dead underrated this one.
  • Greenbank Park – Smaller, peaceful park near the uni area, nice for a break between lectures or walks with the dog.

Beatles Landmarks

You can’t come to Liverpool and not run into something Fab Four related – here’s a few of the best-known spots.

  • The Cavern Club – Legendary basement venue where The Beatles played nearly 300 times – still has live music most nights.
  • Penny Lane – Yes, it’s a real place! Go see the famous sign and grab a coffee at Penny Lane Coffee House.
  • Strawberry Field – Now open to visitors with a lovely little café and exhibition. John Lennon used to play here as a kid.
  • Beatles Statue (Pier Head) – Life-size bronze figures right by the river – free and always popular for a photo.
  • The Beatles Story Museum – Fully immersive museum at the Albert Dock – proper day out for fans.
