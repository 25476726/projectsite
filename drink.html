Pubs & Bars

Struggling to decide where to go? Try our Liverpool City Centre Pub/Bar Picker!

What group of boozers would you like to visit today? Tiers are: A (Our favourites), B, C, D, E, F, U (Our least favourites), W (Wetherspoons)

Tier placements are subject to change, mainly depending on personal experience and is totally subjective! These rankings are just our opinions! The rankings are based on a criteria that consists of:

  • Interior/atmosphere
  • Value for money
  • Selection of beverages
  • Quality of cocktails (if served)
  • Quality of food (if served)
  • Cleanliness of premises
  • Cleanliness and condition of toilets
  • Location
  • Friendliness of staff & bouncers
  • Type of crowd
  • Type of music played and if it's relevant to the bar
  • Volume of music played
  • Any extra facilities such as pool tables or games being present
  • Non alcoholic options

e.g If you would only like A rated boozers, type A. If you would like from A to C type AC. If you want all boozers to be included type all. If you're skint and only want to visit Wetherspoons pubs, type W




You must have at least one drink in each location! If you got multiple of the same place, you must have that number of drinks in that location!

Making your own mind up instead? Here is a selection of some of our favourite places to grab some drinks in Liverpool City Centre!

View our interactive map of all pubs and bars in/around Liverpool! Alternatively you can keep scrolling to see some of our top rated spots!

Head of Steam

Sound spot, this one. Grab a pint or a craft beer and soak up the laid-back vibes. A perfect mix of history and modern, just a short walk from the waterfront.

The Bridewell

Ah, The Bridewell – lovely little boozer with a cracking menu, and you can’t beat the atmosphere. Whether you’re in for a proper pint or a bite to eat, you’ll feel right at home here.

Liverpool Gin Distillery

If you like your gin, this is the place for you. The distillery’s a class spot to sample some local gin – fancy a tipple or a tour? This’ll sort you right out, no bother.

Bobo

Bobo’s got the Spanish vibes. Whether you're in for cocktails or a cheeky night out with the mates, it’s all class here. Dead chilled.

The Railway (Tithebarn St)

Proper old-school boozer with a modern twist. Perfect for grabbing a pint after work or meeting the lads for a catch-up. Always buzzing, and the pints are spot on!

Be at One (Seel Street & Victoria Street)

Class cocktails, great tunes, and a lively crowd. Be at One is all about the good times – whether you’re starting a night out or keeping it rolling 'til late. Get your mates together and enjoy the madness!

Pins Social Club & The Roof (above Pins)

Pins is where it’s at – if you’re after a game of bowling with a drink in hand, this place nails it. Plus, the roof bar's one of the best sun traps in the city, perfect for a chill one with mates.

Brass Monkey

If you’re in the mood for a laugh, Brass Monkey is the place. Proper lively crowd, great drinks, and a solid playlist. You can’t go wrong for a fun night out.

The Merchant

The Merchant’s got it all – chilled vibes, banging cocktails, and the kind of place you can spend hours in without noticing the time fly. Top spot for a relaxed evening out with your crew.

Albert's Schloss

This one’s a bit of a gem. It’s got that big pub feel but with the added bonus of a proper German beer hall. You’ll be sat sipping steins, having a laugh, and feeling like you’re on holiday. You’ll love it!

Lounge 69

Low-key vibes and a bit of a hidden gem. Lounge 69 is a great place for a laid-back night out, enjoying cocktails and some proper tunes. Ideal for those who like their nights chilled.

Ship and Mitre

Classic Liverpool pub with top-quality cask ales. If you want to find a proper local spot with friendly faces, Ship and Mitre's got you covered. A pint here, and you’ll feel right at home.

The Denbigh Castle

This place has a real community feel. Whether you’re popping in for a quick pint or staying for the night, it’s always warm and welcoming. You’ll end up making a few new mates here for sure.

The Saddle

The Saddle's got a cozy vibe. Come in, grab a pint, and relax while you watch the match or have a laugh with mates. It’s a proper local gem for a down-to-earth night out.

Castle Street Townhouse

If you’re after a touch of class, Castle Street Townhouse is spot on. Great for a night out with a bit more style, without feeling too posh. Definitely one to hit if you're looking to impress.

Soho

Vibrant, fun, and always buzzing. Soho's the perfect spot to kick off a night out, with tasty cocktails and a DJ setting the mood. You won’t want to leave once you’re in!

Modo

If you're up for a big night, Modo’s where it’s at. With an electric atmosphere and a top range of drinks, you’ll be dancing the night away in no time.

The Cavern Pub/Club

One of the most iconic spots in Liverpool. Whether you’re a fan of the Beatles or just love a good night out, The Cavern’s atmosphere is unbeatable. You can’t come to Liverpool and not check it out.

Roxy Ballroom (Hanover Street)

Roxy's a laugh, isn’t it? You’ve got ping pong, shuffleboard, and a load of drinks to get stuck into. Perfect spot for a bit of healthy competition and a proper fun night out with the mates.

Spanish Caravan

Nice vibe here, like you’ve just stepped into the Med. Great cocktails and tasty tapas, all with that lively, welcoming atmosphere. It’s got the perfect mix of chilled and lively.

NQ64

Retro vibes all the way – NQ64’s a proper laugh with the old-school arcade games and ace cocktails. Perfect if you’re after a night that’s a bit different with the crew.

Lime Street Central

Lime Street Central’s dead handy, right in the heart of town. Always buzzing with a cracking selection of drinks. Ideal for kicking off a night out or just enjoying a pint with mates.

Metrocola

If you’re after a bit of Caribbean sunshine, Metrocola’s your spot. Top food, great cocktails, and a vibe that makes you feel like you’re on holiday – sound, that!

McGuffie's

McGuffie’s is a proper local – relaxed, friendly, and always got a good pint on the go. Perfect if you’re just looking to unwind with a few mates.

Mean Eyed Cat

Trendy and laid-back, Mean Eyed Cat’s a great spot to chill out. Grab a drink, put your feet up, and unwind with the crew in a class setting.

The Red Lion

The Red Lion’s a dead sound pub. A classic spot with cracking ales, perfect for when you fancy a proper pint in a friendly, no-nonsense pub.

Brewdog

Craft beer lovers, this one’s for you. Brewdog’s all about great beers, laid-back vibes, and a cracking place to catch up with mates over a proper pint.

Doctor Duncan's

Doctor Duncan’s got a bit of everything – good drinks, entertainment, and a lively crowd. A solid spot for anyone looking to have a laugh and enjoy a night out.

McCooley's (Concert Square & Mathew St)

McCooley’s is always a good shout – loads of drinks on offer, a fun atmosphere, and always packed with good vibes. A top place for a night out with your mates.

Skint? Here are all Wetherspoons pubs in and around Liverpool!

The Lime Kiln (Lloyds Bar) L1 Concert Square

The Lime Kiln’s sound if you’re in Concert Square. A classic Wetherspoons with cheap drinks and a lively vibe – perfect for a pre-night out pint or a quiet one with mates.

The North Western, Lime Street Station

Right next to Lime Street station, The North Western is a solid spot for a quick pint before your train or to just take the edge off a hectic day in town.

The Welkin, Matthew Street

The Welkin’s a proper Scouse classic. Dead central, with all the cheap pints you could want and always buzzing with the sounds of a good night out on Matthew Street.

The Captain Alexander, James Street

The Captain Alexander is perfect for a pint before you head off to the docks or for a chill one after work. You know what you're getting – cheap, cheerful, and a decent atmosphere.

The Richard John Blacklers, Charlotte Street

The Richard John Blacklers is a beast of a building. Old school but with a solid vibe inside. A proper Wetherspoons experience, perfect for when you’re skint but still wanna go out.

The Fall Well, Queens Square Bus Station

If you're catching a bus from Queen’s Square, why not pop into The Fall Well for a pint? Proper Wetherspoons vibe with cheap drinks and a casual atmosphere – great for a quick pit stop.

The Thomas Frost, County Road L4

The Thomas Frost is out in the L4 – a bit quieter than the city centre, but perfect for getting stuck into a few drinks without the hustle and bustle of town. Proper local spot.

The Raven, Walton Vale L9

The Raven’s your typical Wetherspoons – cheap pints, good food, and a proper down-to-earth vibe. Ideal if you’re just looking for a quiet pint or a catch up with mates.

The Queen's Picture House, Waterloo

The Queen’s Picture House is a proper gem in Waterloo. It’s got that relaxed, chilled vibe perfect for having a couple of pints and watching the world go by.

The Frank Hornby, Maghull

The Frank Hornby’s a cracking spot for a pint if you’re in Maghull. Dead chilled atmosphere, with cheap drinks and a sound crowd. Can’t go wrong.

The Lifeboat, Formby

The Lifeboat’s a good place if you’re in Formby – nothing fancy, just a solid Wetherspoons. Cheap pints, decent food, and a great place to stop after a day on the beach.

The Court Leet, Ormskirk

The Court Leet in Ormskirk is a right laugh. Good for when you’re on your way through Ormskirk or just fancy a casual drink somewhere cheap and cheerful.

The Navigator, Old Swan

The Navigator’s a great stop if you’re in Old Swan. Classic Wetherspoons – cheap pints, decent grub, and always a friendly crowd. Can’t go wrong with this one.

The Childwall Five Ways Hotel

The Childwall Five Ways Hotel’s a decent spot to grab a cheap pint. Nothing fancy, just good old-fashioned Wetherspoons service and a relaxed vibe.

The Watch Maker, Prescot

The Watch Maker in Prescot’s a right sound place. Proper local, with all the usual Wetherspoons charm – cheap, cheerful, and ideal for a pint after a busy day.

The Bakers Brewery, Huyton

The Bakers Brewery’s sound in Huyton – perfect if you're out in the area. Classic Wetherspoons – great for a cheap pint and a relaxed atmosphere. Can’t go wrong.
