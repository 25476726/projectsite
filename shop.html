Shopping

Liverpool’s got something for everyone when it comes to shopping – from designer brands to boss bargains, local gems to high-tech gadgets. Whether you’re after a fresh trackie, a new telly, or just fancy a mooch, here’s the lowdown on where to go.

Shopping Centres

All your favourite stores in one place – great for a full day out.

  • Liverpool ONE – Massive outdoor shopping centre with big names, restaurants and bars.
  • St John’s Shopping Centre – More budget friendly, proper mix of local and high street names.
  • Metquarter – Fancy indoor centre with more boutique and premium shops.
  • Clayton Square – Small centre near Lime Street with handy high street stores.

Branded Clothing Stores

If you’re into your labels, Liverpool ONE and Church Street are packed with top fashion brands.

  • Superdry – Japanese-inspired streetwear with bold logos and quality jackets.
  • Hollister – Laid-back Cali vibes, great for hoodies and casual fits.
  • Montirex – Liverpool’s own sportswear brand, popular with the young lads.
  • Under Armour – Performance gear for gym heads and footy training.
  • Victoria’s Secret – Lingerie, perfumes, and comfy loungewear.
  • Levi's – Classic denim jeans and jackets with a timeless fit.
  • Hugo Boss – Sharp clobber for going out or dressing smart.
  • North Face – Popular outerwear brand, solid for coats and backpacks.
  • Vans – Skater shoes, graphic tees, and comfy basics.
  • Fred Perry – Iconic polo shirts and mod-style gear.
  • Reiss – Stylish, clean cuts for workwear and smart occasions.
  • Flannels – Designer brands all in one spot, from Stone Island to Off-White.
  • Catwalk – Boutique-style fashion with loads of women’s clothing options.
  • Lucca – Local label with premium menswear and smart casuals.

Trainers & Streetwear

Scousers love a good pair of trainees. These shops are go-to for the freshest kicks and urban style.

  • Size? – Limited edition sneakers and exclusive drops.
  • JD Sports – One of the biggest for Nike, Adidas, and gymwear.
  • Footasylum – Trendy streetwear, trainers, and matching sets.
  • Foot Locker – U.S. trainer classics and sporty styles.
  • Offspring – Known for rare collabs and trainer launches.
  • Kick Game – Premium kicks, including Jordans and Yeezys.

Tech & Electronics

After a new phone, console, or laptop? These stores have you covered.

  • Currys (Aintree) – TVs, washing machines, laptops, all the big tech brands.
  • Apple Store (Liverpool ONE) – Latest iPhones, Macs and helpful service from the Geniuses.
  • Samsung Store – Try out Galaxy phones, tablets, watches and VR gear.
  • EE / Vodafone / Three – Mobile phone contracts, upgrades and accessories.
  • Game – Consoles, video games and merch – great for PS and Xbox gear.
  • CEX – Buy or sell second-hand games, phones, and tech at bargain prices.

Home & Lifestyle

Whether you’re sprucing up the gaff or grabbing essentials, these shops will sort you right out.

  • IKEA (Warrington) – Flat-pack furniture, candles, Swedish meatballs and everything in between.
  • Dunelm – Bedding, curtains, kitchen bits – proper handy for students or first homes.
  • John Lewis – Upscale department store with furniture, fashion and gadgets.
  • Wilko – Cheap and cheerful homeware, DIY, and cleaning stuff (when it's open!).
  • Primark Home – Affordable home decor and bedding in town.
  • H&M Home – Trendy interior bits with a Scandi vibe.

Charity Shops & Bargains

Dead good for a rummage – you never know what you’ll find in these spots.

  • British Heart Foundation – Loads of furniture and electricals for boss prices.
  • Oxfam – Books, vintage clothes, and quirky bits – always a hidden gem.
  • Scope – Clothes, shoes, and accessories – great for a budget fit.
  • B&M – Bargains galore, from snacks to homeware. Boss for a mad shop.
  • Home and Bargains – Local legend – cheap prices on everything under the sun.
  • Poundland – Dead cheap for essentials, snacks, toiletries and bits n bobs.

Geek & Gaming

For the gamers, collectors and comic fans – these are your go-to spots in the city.

  • Game – Consoles, new releases, and second-hand bargains.
  • CEX – Tech trade-ins and pre-owned games for all consoles.
  • Forbidden Planet – Comics, graphic novels, manga and pop culture merch.
  • Liverpool Retro Games – Hidden gem in the city for old school consoles and games.
  • HMV – Music, films, K-pop, vinyls and geeky collectibles.

Vintage & Independent

If you’re after something different or proper unique, these local spots hit different.

  • Resole Vintage – Rare vintage gear and designer finds, dead trendy.
  • 69A – Eclectic antique shop with mad furniture, clothes and collectables.
  • Red Brick Market – Loads of indie stalls under one roof – proper artsy vibe.
  • Lost & Found – Carefully curated vintage and retro fashion.
  • Pop Boutique – Funky vintage clothes and accessories, bang in town.

Local & Niche

Support your locals – these shops are full of character and proper Scouse heart.

  • Utility – Scouse-owned design store with gifts, homeware and cards.
  • R&H Fine Wines – Independent wine shop with loads of character.
  • News From Nowhere – Radical bookshop on Bold Street, run by a collective.
  • Lunya – Deli and Spanish produce shop next to the tapas restaurant.
  • Bold Street Coffee Merch – Beans, gear and local merch from the city’s best brew spot.
