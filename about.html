About Liverpool, & Liverpool's History

General Info about Liverpool

Liverpool is a city situated in the North West of England, approximately 200 miles (by road) from the capitals of England, Scotland and Wales. Liverpool City itself has a population of just under 500,000 people, while the entire metropolitan area has around 2.25 million. Liverpool is the fifth largest metropolitan area in the UK. Liverpool became a borough in the year 1207, founded by king John over 800 years ago! Liverpool then became a city in 1880.

Liverpool's Heritage

In 2008 Liverpool was the European Capital of Culture and prior to this several sites were given World Heritage status, including the Royal Liver Building built between 1908 and 1911, the Albert Dock, and St George's Hall which boasts being the first air conditioned building in the UK. Liverpool is also home to two cathedrals. The Anglican Cathedral, taking almost 75 years to build, which is the largest cathedral in Britain. As well as the Metrapolitan Cathedral, built in the 1960s, which is a very unique landmark in itself.

Liverpool's Football

Football is by far the most popular sport in the city. Liverpool is home to two Premier League sides: Liverpool Football Club (LFC), which was founded in 1892, and is currently located in Anfield. And Everton Football Club (EFC), founded in 1878, and is currently located at Goodison Park in Walton.

A new stadium is nearing completion at Bramley Moore Docks in Vauxhall, which is set to become Everton's home ground in the 2025-2026 season, replacing the old Goodison Park stadium. This new stadium is set to be the 8th largest in England with a capacity of 52,888 people, which is significantly more than the just under 40,000 that Goodison Park holds.

There is a third team located in Merseyside that is far less known about, Tranmere Rovers located in Prenton Park, an area of Birkenhead, located on the Wirral. This team is much less successful, only playing in League 2 (the lowest division of the English Football League). An interesting fact about this team and their ground is that their stadium lights were bought second hand from Everton when they upgraded their lights.

The Grand National, known as "The Races"

The Grand National is an annual horce racing event which takes place in April, around Easter time. This is located at Aintree Racecourse. The Grand National involves three days of horse racing, and individuals are able to bet on which horse will win. It can be watched on TV on ITV Racing, or tickets can be bought to attend this event in person.

Music

Liverpool is very well known as being the World Capital City of Pop, it has been recognised by Guinness World Records for this. By far the most famous group from the city is The Beatles, one of the most successfull Pop music bands.
