LIVERPOOL

Welcome to your ultimate guide to Liverpool, kidda – the city of music, footy, boss nights out, and the friendliest people you’ll ever meet. Whether you’re just popping by for a cheeky weekend, planning a longer stay, or even thinking of calling this place home, we’ve got you covered like a chip butty on a rainy day.

Here you’ll find everything you need to explore the best of what this cracking city has to offer – from its rich and rebellious history, legendary landmarks, and must-see spots (yes, there’s more than just The Beatles!) to the best places for a scran, a bevvy, or a bit of a retail therapy session.

We’ll show you where to grab a proper pint, find the tastiest chippies and restaurants, and point you in the direction of the coolest indie cafes, rooftop bars, and hidden gems only the locals know about. Fancy a bit of shopping? Whether you’re after vintage clobber, big-name brands or quirky Scouse souvenirs – we’ll show you where to go.

There’s also info on how to get about – from Merseyrail to magical ferry rides across the Mersey (cue the soundtrack). You’ll also find top picks for hotels and B&Bs if you’re staying over, and even a look at long-term living if you’re thinking of joining us Scousers permanently.

And don’t worry – if you're not fluent in Scouse yet, we’ve thrown in a handy slang guide to help you talk the talk, and stop you making any dodgy mistakes. We've also provided a boss little quiz to give your scouse skills a test! By the time you’ve had a mooch round this site, you’ll be ordering your next pint like a proper local. So have a nose round, enjoy yourself, and remember… you're always sound in Scouseland. Nice one kidda!
