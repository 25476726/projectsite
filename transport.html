Merseyrail Services

Liverpool's train lines are powered by the Merseyrail service, with brand new state of the art Stadler Class 777 trains, replacing the former Class 507/508 trains following 40 years of service from them. These new trains feature Wi-Fi, USB & plug sockets for charging, and air conditioning for a smooth, comfortable journey! Merseyrail services run every 15 minutes from 6am to 8pm, outside of these times they are every 30 minutes between 5am to 12am. On Sundays, the trains only operate from 8am to 12am and only run every 30 minutes.

From Liverpool, there are Northern Line trains running to Southport, Ormskirk, Kirkby Headbolt Lane, and Hunts Cross. From which there are connecting services to Wigan, Manchester, and Preston.

The Wirral Line trains run to West Kirby, New Brighton, Ellesmere Port, and Chester. From which there are connecting services to Wales, Upton, Heswall, Warrington, Wigan, Manchester, Crewe, Ince, Elton, Stanlow, and Helsby.
