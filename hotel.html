Hotels in Liverpool

Looking for a place to kip in the Pool? Whether you're looking for a plush hotel in the city centre, a quiet countryside getaway, or a budget-friendly option, we've got you covered. Read on to find the perfect gaff to rest your head after a busy day of sightseeing, shopping, or pub crawling in Liverpool.

City Centre Hotels

If you want to stay right in the heart of the action, Liverpool city centre has got a load of sound hotels for all kinds of budgets. Here are some top picks:

  • Hard Days Night Hotel – A Beatle-mania-themed hotel, right by the Cavern Club. Perfect if you're a Beatles fan and want to stay close to the city’s iconic music scene.
  • Malone Lodge Hotel – A proper classic. This Victorian townhouse hotel is ideal for those looking for a bit of luxury and charm, located in a quiet spot just a short walk from all the action.
  • Hotel Indigo Liverpool – Modern, stylish, and located in the heart of the city. It’s ideal for a night out in town, with bars, clubs, and restaurants all on your doorstep.
  • The Shankly Hotel – A hotel with serious footie history, this place is perfect for both football fans and luxury lovers. Right in the city centre, you’ll be close to everything Liverpool has to offer.
  • Jurys Inn Liverpool – A reliable, no-nonsense hotel with spacious rooms and a great location near the docks. Ideal if you're looking for something simple but comfy.

Airbnbs & Cottages Outside the City

If you're after a more peaceful or unique stay outside the hustle and bustle of the city, Liverpool's surrounding areas offer a range of Airbnbs and cottages perfect for a relaxing break.

  • Rural Retreat in Formby – A charming countryside cottage just a short drive from the city. Perfect if you love nature and want to escape to the coast, Formby is known for its beautiful beaches and red squirrels.
  • Secluded Barn in the Wirral – Just across the Mersey, this barn conversion offers a peaceful escape. Surrounded by farmland and woodlands, it’s ideal for a relaxing weekend away.
  • Georgian Country House in West Lancashire – For something a bit more luxurious, this grand house is surrounded by acres of land and offers a real escape from the hustle and bustle.
  • City View Apartment in Toxteth – Fancy a unique stay with views of Liverpool’s skyline? This modern Airbnb offers a cool, urban experience just a stone’s throw from the city centre.

Budget-Friendly Hotels

You don’t need to break the bank to stay in Liverpool. Here are some great budget-friendly options for those looking to save a few quid:

  • YHA Liverpool – Located right by Albert Dock, this youth hostel is a bargain for those who don’t mind sharing. It’s clean, well-located, and cheap – ideal for travellers on a budget.
  • Travelodge Liverpool Central – A basic but reliable option, this Travelodge offers no-frills accommodation right in the city centre. You’ll be close to all the sights without paying through the nose.
  • Premier Inn Liverpool City Centre – Another solid option for budget-conscious travellers, with a great location and comfortable rooms. You’ll be able to get a good night’s sleep without overspending.
  • easyHotel Liverpool – A proper budget gem. Located just off Lime Street, this hotel offers small but comfy rooms for a very affordable price.

Luxury Hotels

If you're looking to treat yourself and enjoy the high life while you're in Liverpool, check out these luxurious hotels:

  • The Titanic Hotel Liverpool – A stunning five-star hotel set in a renovated dock warehouse, offering luxurious rooms and top-notch service. Ideal if you want to experience the best of Liverpool's maritime history.
  • Hilton Liverpool City Centre – A swanky, modern hotel right in the heart of the city. Great for those looking for something plush and upscale, with all the bells and whistles.
  • Hope Street Hotel – This boutique hotel combines style and comfort, located in the cultural quarter of the city. With its sleek design and excellent service, you’ll feel like royalty.
  • 30 James Street – Home of the Titanic – Experience the luxury of staying in a historic building that’s been transformed into a glamorous hotel. The rooms are stunning, and it’s right next to the Albert Dock.

Planning Your Stay

There’s a range of accommodation options in Liverpool for every kind of traveller – from budget hotels and quirky Airbnbs to high-end luxury stays. Whatever your style or budget, you’ll find something to suit your needs.

Book early, especially if you’re coming in the summer or for big events like the Liverpool International Music Festival or the Grand National – hotels fill up quickly!
