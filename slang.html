Don't do this in Liverpool... Unless You're Ready for a Scrap

  • Don't Mention Manchester
  • Scousers love their city fiercely. Comparing Liverpool to Manchester (especially saying it's "better") is a guaranteed way to start a heated conversation. Also never confuse a Scouser with a Manc. It’s basically a sin. Totally different accents, cultures, and footy teams. Know the difference, your life may depend on it. NEVER wear a Man United shirt either. Especially not around Anfield. Or Goodison Park. Actually, best not to pack one at all... you're in Liverpool now, lad.
  • Don't Blag the Scouse Accent
  • You'll just come across like a bad wool, don't even bother. Don't pretend our accent is hard to understand either cause you're talking garbage! The Scouse accent is one of the most unique in the UK. Sure, it’s fast and full of slang, but mocking it or acting confused every time someone speaks will get old very quickly. Best approach? Just listen, ask if you need help once or twice, and enjoy the musical chaos of it.
  • Don't Call It "London Up North"
  • First off, no. Just no. Liverpool is a city with its own soul, history, humour, and swagger... completely different to London in every way that matters. Trying to compare the two or acting like Liverpool is just "cheaper London" is pure ignorance. Prepare for a history lesson (and a scathing put-down) if you come out with that nonsense.
  • Don't Talk Down to Scousers – You’ll Get Schooled Fast
  • Scousers are fiercely proud, dead clever, and have zero patience for snobbery. If you come across like you’re "better" because of your accent, job, or where you’re from, expect to be absolutely rinsed. Humility, humour, and a bit of cheekiness will get you much further than an attitude ever will.
  • Don't Assume Every Scouser is a Football Hooligan
  • Yes, footy is religion around here — but no, we’re not all throwing pints and starting scraps. Scousers know their football inside out and love a good chant, but show a bit of respect (and don't act like you're at a UFC fight) and you'll find yourself in brilliant banter, not bother.
  • Don't Expect Quiet Nights Out
  • Scousers go hard when it comes to nightlife. We're talking all-night raves, mad bar crawls, and outfits that defy weather logic. It’s not a competition, know your limits and hydrate between bevs, unless you fancy ending up in A&E or with a story that starts, “So I woke up in Bootle…” If you're after peace and quiet, maybe head to the countryside like Formby. Or a library.
  • Don't Skip the Locals' Pubs for the Trendy Spots
  • Sure, Baltic Market is sound, but if you’re only hitting up "Insta-famous" spots, you're missing the real Liverpool. The proper boss stories, characters, and pints are found in the backstreet boozers. Step into a proper pub like The Philharmonic or Ye Cracke and you'll see the heart of the city beats strongest over a well-poured pint. Take a look at our boss pubs & bars page to find some more belter spots to go to.
  • Don't Feed the Seagulls Unless You Fancy Losing a Finger (or Your Greggs pasty)
  • Them Liverpool seagulls are not normal birds. They’re massive, fearless, and trained in aerial chip theft. If you wave a sausage roll in the air, don’t be shocked when it’s snatched mid-bite. Keep your scran close, and your pastry closer.
  • Don't Dress for the Weather – Dress for the Occasion (No Matter the Temperature)
  • You’ll see locals rocking shorts, fake tan, and lashes in full force even when it’s 4°C and raining sideways. Scouse fashion is all about commitment. If you turn up to Concert Square in hiking boots and a rain mac, you're getting clocked.
  • Don't Underestimate How Long It Takes to Say Goodbye
  • In Liverpool, saying goodbye isn't a quick "see ya" — it’s a half-hour ritual involving hugs, shouts down the street, a few last-minute jokes, and probably an invite for "one more bevvy." Don't plan a quick Irish goodbye unless you want to be dragged back in for another round!
